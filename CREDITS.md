# Credits

All recorded character voices in Saucer Climb are released under CC0 (public domain). Credit isn't required, but these people made the game sound better, so here they are.

## Big-jump exclamations (Freesound)

- "MaleVoiceWoohoo" by melisandepope. https://freesound.org/people/melisandepope/sounds/557125/
- "woohoo" by unfa. https://freesound.org/people/unfa/sounds/468898/
- "Woo Hoo" by Rocotilos. https://freesound.org/people/Rocotilos/sounds/341488/
- "Joyful - Wouhouh" by Sadiquecat. https://freesound.org/people/Sadiquecat/sounds/801291/
- "Yahoo" by Slimeshop64. https://freesound.org/people/Slimeshop64/sounds/844485/

## The occasional "hee-hee"

- Cut from "HOO! HA! HEE HA!.wav" by Reitanna (Freesound). https://freesound.org/people/Reitanna/sounds/343968/
  The "Hee", a doubled "Hee-hee", and the "Hoo" are short trims of that one recording.

## The Wilhelm scream (one death in ten)

- "S38-01 Man eaten by alligator; screams [Wilhelm screams]" uploaded by craigsmith (Freesound), released as CC0. https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/675810/
  These are takes from the 1951 recording session made for Warner Bros.' "Distant Drums." The CC0 release comes from the uploader; Warner Bros. has never formally confirmed the recording is public domain. If the game is ever sold, swap these for a sound-alike.

## Jump efforts

- **15 vocal male strain/hurt/pain/jump sounds** (OpenGameArt). https://opengameart.org/content/15-vocal-male-strainhurtpainjump-sounds
- "Hup" by MRdeadH (Freesound). https://freesound.org/people/MRdeadH/sounds/613568/
- "jump.wav" by DeltaCode (Freesound). https://freesound.org/people/DeltaCode/sounds/680303/
- **RPG Voice Starter Pack** by CiCiFyre, three female voices (OpenGameArt). https://opengameart.org/content/female-rpg-voice-starter-pack

The clips were trimmed, loudness-matched, and converted to mono MP3. In Saucer mode each species plays them at its own pitch.

The presenter's callouts use your computer's built-in speech voices. Music and all other sound effects are synthesized in the browser by the game itself.
